life-os Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-09

What life-os is

life-os is a personal command-line tool. Yifan Xiao wrote it for personal use. It runs only on the owner's computer. It is not offered to other users.

Google data it uses

  • Read-only calendar access (calendar.readonly): life-os reads the owner's calendars to build a daily schedule summary.
  • Calendars that the app creates (calendar.app.created): life-os creates one secondary calendar and adds, changes, and deletes events only in that calendar.

Storage and sharing

  • The OAuth token and all data stay on the owner's computer, in files that only the owner can read.
  • life-os does not send Google data to any server other than Google's own APIs.
  • life-os does not sell, share, or transfer Google data. It does not use Google data for advertising or to train AI models.

life-os's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoke access

To remove access, open Google Account → Third-party connections and remove life-os. To delete the data, delete the "AI 日程" calendar in Google Calendar.
